About

I started out studying Computer Science in Indonesia, more curious about how systems worked than set on becoming any one kind of engineer. That curiosity is what took me sideways into product and business analysis instead of straight into a dev team.

Over the years that followed I moved between roles that all lived in the same spot: between the people who needed something solved and the people who could build it. I led product delivery for a learning platform used by thousands of students, rebuilt clinic operations around a single source of truth, and helped clients wire up chatbots as an implementation engineer. I was always doing a bit of coding along the way, never quite letting it go.

A few years in, I started missing being a student. I was hungry to learn something new and go deeper on the technical side, so I packed up and went back to school for an MSc in Data Science at Universiteit Leiden.

If there is one thread that runs through all of it, it is that I am genuinely curious about the problem underneath the request, not just the tool used to solve it. That is why I am not precious about the job title: data engineer, implementation engineer, product owner, business analyst, whichever one lets me keep building things and understanding why they matter.
